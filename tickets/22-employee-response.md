# Employee response

## Question
What may an employee response create, and how is it resolved?

## Resolution
An employee may create a clarification, dispute, or new supporting evidence. The original evidence remains immutable. The response can include a source and must link to the original. The manager classifies the response as accepted, partially accepted, not accepted, or needs investigation, and records a reason. An unresolved dispute permits finalization only when the manager acknowledges it and records rationale. HR sees unresolved disputes in audit view.
