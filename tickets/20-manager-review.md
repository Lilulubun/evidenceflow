# Manager review

## Question
What does manager review produce?

## Resolution
The manager opens one employee review, sees Q3–Q4 evidence grouped by competency and separated by self, peer, and manager source, and reviews supporting, conflicting, missing, and unverified evidence. AI drafts a source-cited summary. The manager edits it, records provisional rationale and rating, receives the employee's factual response/context, then finalizes the rating. HR audits process and evidence without approving every rating.
