# HR audit

## Question
What must HR see and control?

## Resolution
HR sees immutable evidence history, linked responses, evidence status, manager classifications and reasons, provisional/final rating history, missing or unsupported evidence, unresolved disputes, and access/timestamp logs. HR may request clarification, flag process violations, reopen a review for correction, and record audit notes. HR cannot silently edit/delete evidence or change a manager rating without a recorded action.
